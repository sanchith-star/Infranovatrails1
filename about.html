OUR APPROACH

Designed as a modular concept.

InfraNova explores a system in which camera observations can be analysed by an AI model, associated with available location details, and organised into reports. Future integrations could include connected roadside monitoring points and a mobile inspection robot.

Each module would need to be developed and tested before being relied upon in real-world infrastructure monitoring.

Our commitment: InfraNova presents a technology concept and proposed system architecture. We do not claim that every described feature is already operational or deployed on public roads.
