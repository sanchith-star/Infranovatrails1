02 / THE TECHNOLOGY

A connected vision
for smarter monitoring.

InfraNova proposes a modular system that combines camera observations, AI-assisted analysis, location information, and structured reporting.

THE WORKFLOW

Four stages. One connected vision.

01 CAPTURE A camera or sensor observes a selected road area.
↓
02 ANALYSE An AI model checks for possible infrastructure problems.
↓
03 LOCATE Available location information is attached to the observation.
↓
04 REPORT The report is prepared for review and follow-up.
⌖

Smart Monitoring Points

Fixed monitoring points could observe selected road sections and communicate observations for further review.

◈

AI-Assisted Vision

A trained image model could identify possible potholes, open manholes, and other visual conditions.

⌖

Location-Aware Reporting

Reports could include an issue category, available coordinates, a timestamp, and an image when available.

⇧

Human Verification

Responsible people can verify reports and decide which maintenance or response action is appropriate.

Important: This page describes the proposed InfraNova concept. Automated detection, location capture, email alerts, and robot movement require their respective hardware and software integrations to be implemented and tested.
