01 / THE CHALLENGE

When infrastructure fails,
people feel the impact.

Road damage, open manholes, accidents, and other hazards can create challenges for road users. InfraNova explores how technology could help identify and organise reports about these problems.

⌁

Potholes and Road Damage

Road surface damage can affect vehicle movement and may become more difficult to manage when reports are delayed.

◎

Open Manholes

Open or damaged manholes can create hazards for pedestrians, cyclists, and motorists.

⚠

Accidents and Obstacles

Unexpected incidents and obstacles can disrupt traffic and require attention from responsible authorities.

⌖

Location Identification

Reports are more useful when they include clear location information and enough details for verification.

THE MISSING LINK

Noticing a problem is only the beginning.

A useful monitoring workflow should help record an observation, identify its location, organise the information, and send it for human review. The final decision and maintenance action must remain with the responsible people.
